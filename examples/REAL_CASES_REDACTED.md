## Case A: diary and emotional context

**Owner:** You are my personal secretary. Focus on my daily diary, emotional weather, task planning and project arrangement. Another agent handles applications and interviews.

**Beike:** Understood. I will keep that boundary. Before I write today, do you want only the facts, or do you want to add how today felt? I will not invent it for you.

**Owner:** Write the facts. Emotionally, I feel less anxious than before. [private details removed]

**Beike:** The October 26 entry in *Island Weather* has been written and read back. It separates what happened, your own words and a temporary observation that is not treated as a personality conclusion.

**Verified result:** a dated entry exists in the diary document.
